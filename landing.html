PACE · Chips 2026 Chapter 5

Page-Adaptive, Cache-Anchored Memory Encryption for RISC-V

An open RISC-V memory encryption engine whose masked key core is formally proven DPA resistant. Precomputing the keystream during cache residency cuts eviction latency from 90 to 10 cycles — a 9× improvement.

ARCHER · Computers 2026 Chapter 4

Cycle-Accurate RISC-V Emulator for Side-Channel Research

Boots Linux 6.6 in under three minutes. 3.7× faster than gem5. Matches Chipyard RTL to 9.4% mean deviation. Reproduces ten attacks and seven new defense policies.

Novel SCA · Chips 2026 Chapter 3

23 Side Channels on Open RISC-V Cores

Six novel RISC-V-specific primitives including PMPectre. Measured on six open cores from in-order to wide out-of-order. Confirmed on FPGA silicon with eight RTL mitigations costing ≤0.8% LUT area.

TWIG · IEEE ISED 2026 Accepted

Page-Table-Walker Cache Partitioning for Domain Isolation

Shows RISC-V page-table walker caches leak across domains. TWIG drives displacement from 25/32 to at most 1, and classification to chance, for just 113 cells and no added SRAM.
